How this works

The only device source
The Asset Central Inventory tab of the workbook. A person is here only if it names them on a laptop, iMac or iPad marked Active/Assigned.
Sales and HO
Sales is a job title containing "Financial Advisor". HO is any other job title found. Both are first guessed by matching the name against the monitoring sheet.
No type
No job title was found for that name. Set one here and it sticks through every later import.
Resigned
A tag this register keeps. The workbook does not record it.
Re-importing
Running the import again refreshes names, devices and departments from the workbook and leaves every correction made here alone.
Not used
The Asset Central Inventory (2) tab, whose assignee names were sorted apart from the serials. Assets at any status other than Active/Assigned are left out too.
