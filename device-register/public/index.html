          Backups

          A copy of every record in the register: people, assets, corrections, resigns and the whole change log. It is made while the register keeps running and kept on the server in . Make one before a big change and at the end of each week, and download one now and then to keep somewhere other than this server. The register also makes one by itself every week: when it is opened and the newest backup is a week old.

          Putting a backup back
          1. Stop the register (STOP-RUNNING-ON-SERVER.bat, or end the task in Task Scheduler).
          2. In the register’s folder, rename register.db to register-before-restore.db, and delete register.db-wal and register.db-shm if they are there.
          3. Copy the backup you want into the same folder and rename it register.db.
          4. Start the register again. Everything is as it was when that backup was made; anything done after it is only in register-before-restore.db.

          Your password

          The one everyone signs in with. Changing it signs out every admin immediately, including anybody who has left. That is the point of it. Invite links are not affected and keep working.

          The words people pick from

          Status, asset type and model are picked from these lists rather than typed, which is what stops one thing being counted as two. Adding a word is deliberate and is recorded. A status that is not hardware to hand out stops an assignment unless a ticket number is given. An admin can delete a word that no asset uses.

          When someone resigns

          The status each choice in the resign pop-up writes. Until you pick one, the register uses the closest word already on the status list, and adds the plain word the first time nothing fits.

          Delete a person

          Anybody who holds nothing can be deleted outright, with a ticket number. The change log keeps every line about them, under their name; nothing in it can be deleted.

           

          Records folded into another

          One person entered twice, folded into the record you kept. Putting one back gives it its own row again; the hardware stays where it was moved to.

          How this works

          The only device source
          The Asset Central Inventory tab of the workbook. A person is here only if it names them on a laptop, iMac or iPad marked Active/Assigned.
          Sales and HO
          Sales is a job title containing “Financial Advisor”. HO is any other job title found. Both are first guessed by matching the name against the monitoring sheet.
          No type
          No job title was found for that name. Set one here and it sticks through every later import.
          Resigned
          A tag this register keeps. The workbook does not record it.
          Re-importing
          Running the import again refreshes names, devices and departments from the workbook and leaves every correction made here alone.
          Not used
          The Asset Central Inventory (2) tab, whose assignee names were sorted apart from the serials. Assets at any status other than Active/Assigned are left out too.
